**Engine gap audit — 9 October 2026**

The follow-up to the Yuriko timing report checked whether representative legal plays survive discovery, native choice and payment, stack resolution, zone changes and combat. It reproduced and repaired the following engine gaps:

- Effect-driven free casts omitted temporary face permission for MDFCs and forced the creature half of Adventures. Etali now offers legal spell faces and Adventure choices. Copied-card casts use a native temporary exile copy, clean up unused copies and preserve the optional wording of Mizzix's Mastery, Psionic Ritual and Bloodthirsty Adversary. Spelltwine keeps its mandatory casts.
- The Oracle v89 wrapper restored the printed card definition after unrelated casts, overwriting resolved Morph/Disguise, Prototype and copied characteristics. Restoration now tracks only the exact temporary Harmonize wrapper. Paid and failed Harmonize controls still restore the original definition safely.
- The final priority filter removed instant-speed loyalty granted by Teferi, Master of Time, The Wandering Emperor and an earned Teferi emblem. Grand Abolisher also incorrectly prohibited abilities of creature cards in hand. Native timing grants and legal hand Cycling now survive those filters; ordinary loyalty timing, once-per-turn limits, taxes and battlefield prohibitions remain checked.
- Off-zone X choice and payment lost ability metadata. This let creature-spell-only mana inflate Channel/Cycling choices and rejected actual X-ability payments with Elementalist's Palette. Hand Channel, Cycling and graveyard payment now preserve the applicable cost context; ordinary non-X cycling remains a negative control.
- Outside-zone type grants were incomplete and separate wrappers ignored timestamps. Maskwood Nexus, Arcane Adaptation and Conspiracy now share an ordered type layer. Casts use effective creature types and the applicable controller; a scoped proposed-caster context also supplies the correct Nexus/Horn discount when Gonti permits a paid cast of a foreign-owned card. Ownership and casting permission remain separate. Graveyard-only Ashes of the Fallen changes stay in the graveyard. Herald's Horn, Door of Destinies, Director Nick Fury and Folk Hero use effective types for discounts, counters, retrieval and draw.
- Ayesha Tanaka's target compiler treated stack abilities as permanents and never offered her response. The native stack-ability route now accepts actual artifact activations, including a sacrificed Dispeller's Capsule source, while rejecting creature activations and artifact triggers. Icy Manipulator controls verify both paying and declining the additional white mana.
- Blink, departure, control changes and loss of creature type left stale combat links. Combat removal now clears the departing incarnation and preserves unaffected multi-block assignments and the blocked status of former attackers. First-step eligibility is also recorded before damage: pumping a zero- or negative-power first striker no longer grants a second ordinary damage step. Genuine double strike still works.
