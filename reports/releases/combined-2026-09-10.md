# Combined release — 10–11 September 2026

This release collects the completed card-import, mana-choice and multiplayer-verification work from the shared checkout, based on `a125a78`.

- The [eighteen-card review](../cards/restricted-legacy-2026-09-10/README.md) corrects native rules and enables those existing cards for arbitrary deck import. All **20,962 definitions** are import-eligible; there are still **120 built-in decks**. Twenty-two WebP files supply the eighteen card images and four commander portraits.
- Astral Cornucopia chooses one X for all three symbols, displays the adjusted total, offers explicit mana-source payment and lets its controller choose the produced color on each tap. Tests cover cost reduction, all five colors, changing counter counts and avoiding payment side effects on rejected choices.
- The [Live control review](../multiplayer-controls-2026-09-10.md) verifies the shared controls for two and four human clients and updates the documentation. The browser drivers now start their own isolated local backend; the Astral driver can also verify a deployed URL through `GAME_URL`.
