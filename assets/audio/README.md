# Commander audio

The October 16 pack adds three original instrumental loops, introduces versioned
runtime effects, and adds optional spoken cues made
with Fish Studio's official Sarah voice. The keyword extension adds thirteen
short effects and six comments for strong plays. All assets are served with the game;
playback never contacts an audio provider or requires an account token.

| New instrumental | Character |
| --- | --- |
| Council at Dusk | Soft plucked strings and keys, a quiet mystical council |
| Aether Tides | Airy keys and floating astral ambience |
| Emberwatch | Warm pads and restrained low drums |

The instrumentals and first synthesized effect pack are original deterministic
synthesis, generated by `scripts/generate-original-audio.py`. They contain no sampled soundtrack or
existing melody. Fish Studio provides the spoken cues only: “Your turn,”
“Victory. Well played,” and “Defeat. A new challenge awaits.” The manifest records
voice ID, exact text, source URL and task ID, as well as durations, file sizes and
SHA-256 hashes for all current assets. Legacy effects and their provenance remain
available for older cached clients.

Menu → Music & sound offers six instrumentals, separate music/effects/narration
volumes, mute, and previews. Existing track choices are preserved; Council at
Dusk is the default for new settings. Music defaults to 18%, effects to 38%, and
narration to 0% (off). Turn narration is for the local player only. A single voice
plays at a time, with music briefly lowered while it speaks. Hidden tabs, mute,
save replay and reconnect never replay missed announcements.

New instrumentals use circular ambience and approximately -22 LUFS mastering;
voice cues use -20 LUFS and a -3 dB true-peak target. A browser gesture unlocks
playback. The runtime loads assets on demand, coalesces repeated effects, limits
simultaneous effects, and suspends audio when the page is hidden. Voice and effect
previews obey their own volume controls and the global mute control.

To regenerate the new music/effects, run the deterministic generator with NumPy,
SciPy and FFmpeg installed (see its `--help`). Fish voice sources are permanent
URLs in the manifest; download and master with the recorded FFmpeg filter,
44.1 kHz mono, libmp3lame at 96 kbps. Regenerating speech through Fish Studio is a
separate paid operation; downloading these existing results is sufficient.

Coverage: `tests/game-audio.test.mjs` and `tests/browser/game-audio.mjs` verify
preferences, real MP3 decoding, output, mute/visibility, voice lifecycle, actual
human/AI actions, Live listener ownership and decision preservation.

## Refined effects

The current twelve-effect pack uses `*-v3.mp3` URLs. It adds distinct sorcery,
spell-copy and defeat sounds, with shorter recurring cues and different textures
for counters, casting, combat and major impacts. Victory and defeat follow the
local Solo player or Live viewer; a draw has no win/loss fanfare. The six music
tracks, Fish narration and saved volume choices are unchanged.

The mixer allows three active effects. Major events gently retire less important
tails and briefly protect their onset from smaller ticks. Superseded downloads
are dropped, and repeated previews replace earlier previews. Muting, hiding the
page and changing games cancel queued and loading effects.

Regenerate the current effects with:

```bash
python3 scripts/generate-polished-sfx.py --metadata output/polished-sfx.json
```

The generator uses original deterministic synthesis and local FFmpeg mastering.
The manifest records measurements and hashes; all earlier effect files and their
provenance remain in `legacyTracks` for older cached clients. Listening impressions
are not inferred from the measured loudness, spectral and tail checks.

## Keyword effects and strong-play commentary

Thirteen short effects cover flying, vigilance, haste, menace, reach, first
strike, double strike, trample, lifelink, deathtouch, infect, wither and
proliferate. The runtime plays one representative keyword sound per brief event
batch; major impacts and game results retain priority. Effects follow public
completed events: legal attacks/blocks, positive damage, successful life gain
and actual deathtouch removal. Haste only sounds when it enables an otherwise
summoning-sick attacker. Trample follows positive overflow at its assigned
recipient; an unblocked trampler does not produce that cue.

Six new English comments use the same Fish Studio Sarah voice:

| Event | Condition | Spoken comment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heavy hit | At least 12 actual damage to a player | A devastating hit. |
| Board wipe | At least 5 actual departures from one wipe | The battlefield has changed. |
| Big spell countered | Public countered spell mana value at least 6 | A powerful spell, denied. |
| Huge arrival | Public creature power at least 8 or toughness at least 10 | A formidable presence. |
| Recovery | Gain at least 8 life, starting at 10 or less and ending at 15 or more | Back in the fight. |
| Token army | At least 5 creature tokens created in one batch, excluding spell copies | An army takes shape. |

Enable spoken comments using **Menu → Music & sound → Narration**; its default
remains 0%. Comments share an 18-second cooldown and skip while another line is
playing or loading. Victory/defeat announcements can replace commentary. Audio
never delays a decision; replay, AI simulations and hidden tabs stay silent.
Solo and Live consume immutable public facts rather than mutable card traits.

The current manifest contains 6 music tracks, 25 effects and 9 voice clips.
The keyword assets total about 108 KB and the six comments about 115 KB. They
load on demand; no Fish account, provider request or key is needed for playback.
Generate the original keyword effects with NumPy, SciPy and FFmpeg:

```bash
python3 scripts/generate-keyword-sfx.py --metadata output/keyword-sfx.json
```

The generator imports helpers from `generate-polished-sfx.py`; the manifest
records both source hashes. Keywords use `keyword-*-v1.mp3` paths and preserve
all existing music, effects and settings. Direct Safari testing is unavailable
in this environment; automated playback checks use Chromium.

## Earlier pack provenance

Three original instrumentals and four milestone effects generated through the user's
Higgsfield account on 7 September 2026, plus five action effects generated on
20 September 2026. The model catalog and CLI were checked:
`sonilo_music` generated music and `mirelo_text_to_audio` generated effects.
These use original fantasy prompts. No existing soundtrack was supplied as a
reference or sampled in the asset pipeline.

`manifest.json` records the exact submitted prompts, model, completed job IDs,
generation dates, mastered durations, file sizes and SHA-256 hashes. Raw results are cached in
the ignored `output/game-audio/raw` folder. No provider API or account token is
required by the game; all playback is local, from these MP3 files.

| Instrumental | Character | Loop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moonlit Grove | Felt piano, wooden harp, soft drums | about 88 seconds |
| Astral Library | Dusty keys, celesta, floating pads | about 88 seconds |
| Ember Sanctum | Nylon guitar, dark warm pads, hand percussion | about 88 seconds |

Music uses a baked circular two-second crossfade and -22 LUFS mastering, with a
-3 dB true-peak target. Effects use relative onset trimming, a -20.9 dB RMS
ceiling and a -3.7 dB sample-peak ceiling before MP3 encoding, plus short fades.
The runtime defaults to 18% music and 38% effects, crossfades track changes,
limits concurrent effects and coalesces simultaneous events. Action cues have
0.8–1.2 second cooldowns; milestone cues retain their 2.5-second cooldown. The audio
graph includes a compressor to control overlaps. Device mute/volume settings
are independent of game state and reduced motion.

Menu → Music & sound offers track selection, both volume controls, mute and a
selector for previewing each effect. A browser interaction unlocks playback;
hidden tabs suspend audio.

| Action effect | Trigger |
| --- | --- |
| Combat | A nonempty attack declaration |
| Venture | Entering a dungeon room, including Undercity |
| Counters | Adding public battlefield/player counters or proliferating chosen subjects |
| Counterspell | Successfully countering a spell or ability |
| Instant | Casting an instant; softer/lower variation for sorceries and a higher variation for spell copies |

Spell cues use the public types of the selected spell face, so Adventures and
split cards are classified by what was cast. Face-down spell identities never
select a sound. Repeated counters and simultaneous notices coalesce into one cue.
Live forwards the same public cast, combat and dungeon notifications; joining or
reconnecting does not replay the event backlog.

Existing effects mark commander/powerhouse arrivals (summon), actual combat
damage of 10 or more (heavy impact), actual noncombat damage of 10 or more or a
board wipe affecting at least three cards (explosion), and game end (victory).
Land plays, ordinary permanent casts, smaller hits, prevention and ordinary
removal stay silent. Save replay, AI simulations, hidden
identities and UI rerenders do not trigger sounds.

To master already completed jobs, run `python scripts/generate-game-audio.py`.
It requires NumPy, FFmpeg and network access for uncached result downloads.
`--generate` additionally submits missing Higgsfield jobs and may spend credits;
it resumes completed jobs and uses the account's single-job audio queue. Submitted
job IDs are saved before waiting, so a resumed run does not submit them again.
On Windows the script resolves `higgsfield.cmd` automatically. To regenerate or
master only this action set while preserving the existing assets and manifest:

```powershell
python scripts/generate-game-audio.py --only combat venture counters counterspell instant
```

Add `--generate` only to submit missing jobs on the signed-in account.

Existing coverage: `tests/game-audio.test.mjs` and `tests/browser/game-audio.mjs`.
The browser suite exercises actual paid human/local-AI spells, Stack reviews,
attack declaration, damage/prevention and exile, plus MP3 decoding and the
running Web Audio graph and individual effect previews on desktop and phone
layouts. See `reports/game-audio-2026-09-20.md` for this revision's validation.
